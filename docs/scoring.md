# Job Scoring Rubric

Total score: 100

## Hard filters
Immediate reject / score 0 if:
- unpaid
- requires active clearance or US citizenship as a hard gate
- clearly senior (5+ years and senior scope)
- purely non-technical compliance role without SOC/security operations overlap

## Positive weights
- Title alignment (0-20)
  - SOC Analyst I / Security Operations Analyst / Entry-Level Cybersecurity Analyst: 16-20
  - Adjacent blue-team role: 10-15
  - Secondary target (junior pentest) with realistic asks: 6-12
- Experience level fit (0-15)
  - Entry-level / associate / 0-2 years: 12-15
  - 3 years: 6-10
- Sponsorship / OPT friendliness (0-20)
  - Explicit sponsorship/OPT friendly: 18-20
  - Silent/unknown: 8-12
  - Negative language: reject or 0-3
- Skills match (0-20)
  - Wazuh/SIEM/log analysis/security monitoring/incident response/NIST/GRC overlap
- Location fit (0-10)
  - Remote US: 10
  - Saint Louis hybrid/onsite: 9-10
  - US relocation: 7-9
- Salary fit (0-10)
  - >= 65k: 10
  - unknown: 5
  - below 65k: 0-3
- Company preference fit (0-5)
  - MSSP/mid-size cyber/cloud/consulting: 3-5

## Resume tailoring target
- Tailor only when job score >= 85 and Sai replies YES.
- Tailored resume should improve keyword relevance and role framing while staying fully defensible.
- Target tailored score >= 95 where truthful.
